# Trademarks

Yantra's source code is released under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). The licence covers the
code; it does not grant rights to the **Yantra name**, the **Yantra logo** or the "Made with
Yantra" badge artwork (together, the "marks"). This page explains how you may use them.

## You may

- Use, modify and redistribute the code under the MIT licence, including in commercial
  products.
- Say that your project uses or is built with Yantra, and link to
  <https://github.com/niyankhadka/yantra>.
- Keep the default "Made with Yantra" badge in diagrams you produce.
- Use the name and logo, unchanged, in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews that refer to
  this project.

## Please don't

- Use the name or logo for a fork, a modified version, or another product or service in a
  way that suggests it is the official Yantra or is endorsed by its maintainer.
- Publish packages, extensions or apps named "Yantra" (or something confusingly similar)
  that are not from this project.
- Change the logo, or combine it with your own marks.

If you fork Yantra and distribute your changes, give your fork its own name and logo. You may
still say it is "based on Yantra".

## Questions

For permission or questions about the marks, open an issue at
<https://github.com/niyankhadka/yantra/issues> or contact the maintainer,
[@niyankhadka](https://github.com/niyankhadka).

This page is a statement of the maintainer's policy, not legal advice.
